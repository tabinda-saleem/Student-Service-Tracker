ABOUT OUR PROJECT

Making student services easier to manage.

Student Service Tracker is a student-focused portal designed to organize service requests and improve communication.

OUR PURPOSE

Less confusion. More clarity.

The problem: students often visit or contact several offices just to ask where their service request stands, and staff have no single place to manage the requests they receive.

Our solution: one portal where students apply, staff review and update the status, and admins manage services and assignments.

WHO USES IT

Three roles, one workflow.

◎
STUDENT

Apply and track

Browse services, submit requests with documents, follow the status, read notifications and give feedback.

✓
STAFF

Review and process

Open assigned requests, check documents, add remarks and update the request status.

⚙
ADMIN

Manage the portal

Manage users, services and departments, assign requests to staff and view reports.

REQUEST LIFECYCLE

From submission to completion.

Every request moves through the same stages so everyone sees the same status.

Submitted→Under Review→Verified→Processing→Completed

A request can also be marked Need More Information, Rejected or Cancelled.
